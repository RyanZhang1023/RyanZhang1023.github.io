Pocket Prism

“Pocket Prism” is an original funk piece inspired by Cory Wong’s live show at Shanghai’s Bandai Namco Dream Hall on June 11, 2024. Drawing inspiration from its groovy bassline and stylistic brass, I composed a similar piece trying to replicate the rhythmic feature of funk music while adding dynamics via chorus. Instrumental layering is achieved using electric piano and brass, which specifies the chord progression. For the guitar, I experimented with shredding to craft intricate melodies. Together, this piece captures the rhythmic groove and infectious energy of funk.
