Death by Glamour

Tags: Transcription, Techno

This is a transcription of Toby Fox's techno piece "Death by Glamour," which is the 68th track in the UNDERTALE Soundtrack. I used Serum to replicate the synth leads and bass by adjusting the waveform of the oscillators, automating cutoffs using LFOs, and adding effects. I applied Keyscape to transcribe the piano by adding a high pass and decreasing the reverb. I implemented Halion Sonic's Orchestra Hit throughout to reproduce the musical effect that Fox used. I used Kontakt for the alto sax and Groove Agent for the drum kit, capturing the techno elements of the beat.
