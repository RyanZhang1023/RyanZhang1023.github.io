LOST IN RIO — GUITAR COVER GUTHRIE GOVAN

Background / 01

How a love of hard rock opened the door to jazz and bossa nova.

Why this piece

Although I first fell in love with hard rock, learning the guitar opened my ears to the richness of other genres, from jazz to bossa nova. One piece that particularly inspired me was Guthrie Govan’s “Lost in Rio,” which fuses jazz harmonies with Brazilian rhythms.

Challenge / 02

Recreating intricate guitar lines through steady practice.

What it took

Recreating its intricate guitar lines—full of slides, ornaments, and subtle nuances—was a formidable challenge at first. Yet through steady practice, I not only mastered its technical demands but also captured the song’s effortless blend of warmth, rhythm, and carefree spirit.
