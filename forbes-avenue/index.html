Background / 01

A small musical document of a major change in my everyday life.

A place becoming familiar

I composed Forbes Avenue during my first month at Carnegie Mellon University, when I was adjusting to life away from Shanghai. Moving from a bustling city to the quieter atmosphere of Pittsburgh gave me an opportunity to live independently and reconsider who I am, what I enjoy, and what kind of life feels right for me.

I wanted the instrumental to capture the tranquility of ordinary moments. Its layered guitars move between intimacy and fullness, reflecting the mixture of solitude, nostalgia, uncertainty, and quiet contentment that accompanied this transition.

Rather than describing the city directly, I used musical texture and environmental sound to suggest what it felt like to gradually become familiar with a new place.

Production / 02

Building a wall
of sound.

Recording and arrangement

I recorded ten guitar tracks using my Fender Telecaster Deluxe and shaped their tone with the Neural DSP Cory Wong Archetype plugin. I used EQ to bring out the low-mid frequencies and applied compression where needed.

In the chorus, doubled guitar parts are panned fully left and right to widen the stereo image and create a fuller wall of sound. More intimate passages provide contrast. I also routed the guitars through a shared reverb bus, adjusting the wet/dry balance to create cohesion without allowing the midrange to become muddy.

Finally, I reversed a guitar recording to introduce an atmospheric texture, adding movement between the more clearly articulated guitar phrases.

Field recording / 03

ENVIRONMENTAL AUDIO · PEDESTRIAN TRAFFIC WARNING

Sound and place

To ground the composition in its setting, I sampled the pedestrian traffic warning, “Wait, wait to cross Morewood Avenue at Forbes Avenue.” The announcement is an ordinary part of moving through Pittsburgh, but it became a recognizable marker of my new surroundings.

Placing this fragment alongside the layered guitars blurs the boundary between composed music and environmental sound. The sample connects the instrumental to a specific place without turning it into a literal soundscape. It captures the small, everyday details through which an unfamiliar city gradually starts to feel like home.

Reflection / 04

A soundtrack
to transition.

What the piece means

Forbes Avenue is both a musical experiment and a personal snapshot of the first weeks of a new chapter. The guitar layers express the emotions of that transition, while the traffic announcement anchors those emotions in the physical environment around me.

The result is a piece about how a place becomes familiar: not through a single defining moment, but through repeated encounters with the sounds, streets, and quiet routines of everyday life.
