From the Soil

This work is inspired by Fei Xiaotong's classic sociological work From the Soil: The Foundations of Chinese Society, constructing an auditory experiment by fusing elements of Chinese ethnic music and Western symphonic music. The lyrics in the song "Waiting for Me" and "My Home is Above the Clouds" express the migration of rural populations to cities for work in the context of China's rapid urbanization, as well as the longing for rural hometowns and the exploration of identity and belonging. This emotion encompasses nostalgia for traditional rural culture and reflects the crisis of belonging brought about by increased population mobility in modern society. I mixed the original rural samples and audio materials (over 50 tracks), improving the volume, stereo image, and EQ of various instruments to expand the sound field, especially the symphonic elements, and added reverb, delay, and other effects to some audio to enrich the timbre.
