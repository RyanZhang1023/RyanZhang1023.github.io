From the Soil

“From the Soil” is an independent mixing project of a song that fused elements of Chinese ethnic music and Western symphonic music. I mixed the original rural samplings and audio materials (over 50 tracks), improving the volume, stereo image, and EQ of various instruments, especially the symphonic elements. Added reverb, delay, and overdrive to some audio to enrich the timbre. The result mix captures the nostalgia for traditional rural culture and reflects the crisis of belonging brought about by increased population mobility in modern society.
